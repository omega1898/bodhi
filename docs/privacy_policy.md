# Privacy policy: Until We Find Home

*Last updated: November 2026*

**Until We Find Home collects no personal data.**

- The game does not use the internet. It sends nothing anywhere.
- Your progress (which chapters are unlocked, and which keepsakes you've found) is saved only on your device, so you can carry on where you left off. Deleting the app deletes it.
- There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- The game does not use your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change is released.
